# MOB Business Strategy

MOB stands for Mobile Operated Business. The product should grow from offline POS into a one-stop retail management app for small businesses, local shops, and retail counters.

## Target Audience

- Kirana stores and grocery shops.
- General stores and stationery shops.
- Mobile accessory shops and clothing boutiques.
- Small wholesalers and family-run businesses.
- Solo shopkeepers who need billing, stock, and reports without a costly POS setup.
- Small pharmacies later, only after legal and compliance scope is handled.

## Pain Points

- Manual notebooks cause stock mistakes and missed sales history.
- Shopkeepers lose time calculating totals, discounts, and payment summaries.
- Stock-outs are discovered too late.
- UPI and cash totals are hard to reconcile at the end of the day.
- Expensive POS systems feel risky, complex, or internet-dependent.
- Many apps look untrustworthy or too technical for a shop counter.

## Why They Would Pay

- Faster daily billing.
- Fewer stock losses and surprises.
- Cleaner PDF/WhatsApp invoices.
- Better cash/UPI/card reporting.
- Confidence that business data survives through backup/export.
- Advanced reports that clearly improve purchasing and stock decisions.

## Why They Would Not Pay

- Fear of complicated setup.
- Distrust of subscriptions.
- Low willingness to pay before habit is formed.
- Concern that the app will stop working without internet.
- Concern that payment will interrupt billing during customer rush.

## Overcoming Reluctance

- Keep a generous free plan.
- Never interrupt active billing with a hard paywall.
- Use soft limits and usage counters.
- Explain value before asking for payment.
- Offer yearly discounts and a lifetime early-supporter option.
- Make data export available so users do not feel trapped.

## Offline-First Value Proposition

MOB should be usable during poor internet, power cuts, and shop rush. Billing, inventory, reports, receipts, and local backup must work offline. Cloud backup and sync can be future upgrades, but the shop counter flow must not depend on them.

## India-Specific Selling Points

- Cash/UPI/card tracking.
- WhatsApp-friendly invoices.
- Simple GST-ready path later.
- Low-cost yearly pricing.
- One-time lifetime option for early adopters.
- Fast startup and low-end Android support.

## Future Global Positioning

Globally, MOB can be positioned as a lightweight offline-first retail OS for micro-retailers: POS, inventory, customer ledger, supplier ledger, expenses, analytics, backup, and receipts from a phone.

## Launch Strategy

1. Start with local shopkeeper trials.
2. Offer the free plan with clear limits.
3. Collect feedback on billing speed, invoice trust, and stock workflow.
4. Add paid upgrades only after the free workflow feels reliable.
5. Use short demo videos for billing, stock, reports, and backup.

## App Store Pitch

MOB is a simple retail POS and inventory app for small shops. Create bills, manage products, track stock, view sales reports, and share professional invoices from your phone, even without internet.

## Suggested Onboarding

- Pick business type.
- Enter business name and phone.
- Add first product manually or by barcode.
- Create a demo bill.
- Show where reports, backup, and settings live.
- Explain free plan limits gently.

## Future Monetization Architecture

- Plan metadata in app code.
- Usage counters from SQLite.
- Feature flags per plan.
- Gentle upgrade prompts near limits.
- Upgrade-later flow.
- No payment gateway until pricing is validated.
