# MOB Product Positioning

## Product Name

MOB - Retail POS & Inventory

Meaning: Mobile Operated Business.

## One-Line Positioning

MOB is an offline-first retail POS and inventory app for small shops that need fast billing, stock control, and professional invoices from a phone.

## Who It Is For

- Kirana and grocery counters.
- Family-run stores.
- Local retail shops.
- Solo shopkeepers.
- Small wholesalers.
- Mobile accessory, clothing, and stationery shops.

## Core Promise

Fast billing, reliable stock, clean invoices, useful reports, and offline operation without expensive setup.

## Trust-Building Strategy

- Professional invoice design.
- Clear local data ownership.
- Manual backup/export.
- No forced cloud account at startup.
- Gentle paid prompts only after value is visible.
- Transparent free plan limits.

## Differentiators

- Offline-first.
- Low-end Android focus.
- Shop-counter speed.
- India-friendly cash/UPI/card thinking.
- Generous free limits.
- Future GST, printer, ledger, and backup upgrades.

## Future Feature Positioning

MOB can become a one-stop retail app:

- POS and invoice.
- Inventory and stock purchase.
- Customer and supplier ledger.
- Expenses and profit reports.
- Thermal printing.
- GST-ready billing.
- Google Drive/cloud backup.
- Multi-device sync.
